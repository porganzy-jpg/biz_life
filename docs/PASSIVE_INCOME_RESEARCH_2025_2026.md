## TIER S: Best ROI for Developer with $1,500

### 1. Micro SaaS Products

**The single best option for a developer.** Build a small software product that solves one specific problem for a niche audience.

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Realistic Monthly Income** | $0-5,000+ MRR (39% of indie SaaS founders are solo) |
| **Initial Capital Required** | $0-200 (hosting costs only) |
| **Time to Set Up** | 2-8 weeks to MVP |
| **Ongoing Maintenance** | 5-10 hrs/week |
| **Risk Level** | LOW (only time investment) |
| **Fully Automatable** | 80-90% after launch |

**Real Examples:**
- Solo dev portfolio earning $48K/year combined from two micro-SaaS tools
- Senja.io (testimonial collection) hit $1M ARR from one niche pain point
- 70%+ profit margins are typical for bootstrapped micro SaaS
- Wave of solo founders hitting $5K/month by stacking multiple small products

**Best Approach for $1,500:**
- Use free tier hosting (Vercel, Railway, Supabase)
- Spend $1,500 on domain + initial marketing only
- Target B2B niches (higher willingness to pay)
- Stack: FastAPI/Next.js + PostgreSQL + Stripe

**Why It's #1:**
- Recurring revenue (subscribers pay every month)
- Compounds over time (unlike trading which resets)
- Your coding skills ARE the product
- $0 marginal cost per user
- Can sell the business later for 3-5x annual revenue
